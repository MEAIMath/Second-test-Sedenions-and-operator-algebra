## 11. Discussion and scope

**Relation to the published program.** The destination — $G_{SM}$ from $J_3(\mathbb{O})$ — is Dubois-Violette–Todorov's, in Boyle's idempotent-stabilizer form, generalized by Baez–Schwahn. **The fork, held open by design.** The transported chirality grading turns out to be $\mathfrak{su}(2)_{\mathrm{weak}}$'s own Cartan axis — an object with no Lorentz pedigree. *Reading A (transmuted)*: the weak sector does not couple to a chirality; it contains one as an axis of its own quaternionic action. *Reading B*: the physical $\gamma^5$-analogue is a different object, living, if anywhere in this corpus, at the fermionic state level where Lorentz structure enters. The $\gamma$-ambiguity of §9 is prior to this question, not a resolution of it. Nothing in this paper decides the fork, and the two readings are recorded at equal rank with B's evidence currently heavier.

**Open, named.** Canonical selection: whether the construction's own objects select a $\gamma$-anchor (and hence a chirality-sensitivity) rather than leaving it external. The electroweak mixing angle: a reason for the Weinberg combination beyond $Q=T_3+Y$ landing correctly. The fermionic program (quasi-free states on the $\mathrm{Cl}(0,8)$ module, a state-dependent chirality-odd antiunitary, quantization of this census): the named frontier, deliberately not begun here.
