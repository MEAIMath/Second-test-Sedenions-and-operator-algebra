## The root: G3 seen symplectically

The kernel-invariance criterion (inventory G3) — a canonical diagonal anchor's tunnel kernel is $J_0$-invariant iff $L_c,L_a$ annihilate it jointly — has a symplectic reading that resolves this entire correspondence in two mechanisms rather than one. **Invariance $\Rightarrow$ nondegeneracy is a theorem, not an observation**: for any $J_0$-invariant subspace and nonzero $v$ in it, $J_0v$ is also in the subspace, and $\sigma(v,J_0v)=g(v,v)>0$ by the compatible-triple relation ($g(x,y):=\sigma(x,J_0y)$, Foundation's own definition) — $\sigma$ cannot vanish identically on an invariant subspace, so invariance forces nondegeneracy outright. **The converse — that $J_0$-transversality forces the Lagrangian property — is not a general symplectic fact; it is verified per-anchor below**, derived exactly for two of the three tested classes. Since full-band $J_0$-invariance is precisely what separates the 24 extreme-class anchors from the 60 generic ones at the kernel (G3's own scope), the correspondence's mechanism is class-dependent by a criterion this note extends beyond the kernel to the full band structure — confirmed directly on all three integrated anchors, derived rather than merely observed on two of the three classes (below).

## Mechanism 1: the generic class pairs across the seam

**[FACT, two-implemented] For the expanding anchor ($e_1+e_{10}$), each of the three $T_A$-bands is $J_0$-transverse, and the drift/diffusion-invariant decomposition into band 2 and the coupled kernel+band-4 sector is a genuine Lagrangian pair.** Checked directly: $\Omega$ restricted to either sector alone is exactly zero (residuals $10^{-17}$–$10^{-33}$), while the cross-block pairing $B$ is invertible ($\det B=-1$). Since $\sigma(t)$ stays block-diagonal under this same decomposition (confirmed: the $A,D$ cross-term between sectors is exactly zero), the symplectic spectrum of the full system is not a property of either block — it is a **geometric mean across the pairing**:
$$\nu(t)^2 = \mathrm{eig}\big(\sigma_2(t)\cdot B\cdot\sigma_c(t)\cdot B^T\big).$$
Tested against the measured branches at $t=3$: $\{2.3732,1139.0643\}$ against $\{2.373,1139.067\}$ — exact to four decimals, no free parameter. This is why both prior stories died: the slow branch (2.373, refusing to be $e^3$ or $e^6$) was never one sector's own exponential — it is the paired product of a growing factor and a lingering one, invisible to any reading that looked at a single side of the seam.

## Mechanism 2: the extreme classes decompose per band

**[THM, two-implemented] Full-band $J_0$-invariance is derived, not merely observed, on both extreme classes — by two different arguments.** For the **pure-antilinear class** ($e_1+e_{12}$): $\{L_A,J_0\}=0$ exactly (verified, residual $0.0$) — $L_A$ itself anticommutes with $J_0$. Since $J_0$ is antisymmetric, $L_A^T$ inherits the same anticommutation, and two anticommutations compose to a commutation: $T_AJ_0=L_A^TL_AJ_0=L_A^T(-J_0L_A)=-(L_A^TJ_0)L_A=-(-J_0L_A^T)L_A=J_0T_A$. Every $T_A$-eigenspace is therefore automatically $J_0$-invariant — a two-line theorem, confirmed exactly ($\|[T_A,J_0]\|=0.0$). For the **mixed extreme class** ($e_4+e_9$): the same anticommutation does not hold for $L_A$ itself ($\|\{L_A,J_0\}\|\approx9.8$, genuinely nonzero), so $[T_A,J_0]=0$ is not automatic the same way — but expanding $T_A=L_c^TL_c+L_a^TL_a+L_c^TL_a+L_a^TL_c$ against $J_0$ shows $[T_A,J_0]=0$ is exactly equivalent to the cross-Gram condition $L_c^TL_a+L_a^TL_c=0$, confirmed directly (residual $0.0$) — a new, small, exact fact about this class, not previously stated. (For the **generic class**, by contrast, $\|[T_A,J_0]\|=8.0$ exactly — genuinely nonzero, consistent with the Lagrangian-pair mechanism above rather than per-band invariance.)

**[FACT, two-implemented] All three $T_A$-bands are individually $J_0$-invariant and $\Omega$-nondegenerate** — each is a genuine, self-contained little phase space. Verified directly on the contracting anchor ($e_4+e_9$): $\det(\Omega\text{ restricted})=1.0$ on all three bands. The full correspondence is then simply the restricted symplectic spectrum computed independently on each band, with no pairing needed:

| Band | Dimension | Restricted symplectic spectrum at $t=3$ | Identification |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kernel ($T_A=0$) | 4 | $1.5$ | The frozen core, exact stasis |
| $T_A=2$ | 8 | $4.5$ | The diffusive, linearly-growing branch |
| $T_A=4$ | 4 | $0.5$ | The vacuum-landed, decaying branch |

All three match the anchor's own measured branches exactly. The three-way character of the contracting anchor's own flow — frozen, growing, decaying — is not three coincidental behaviors; it is three independent phase spaces, one per band, each evolving under its own restricted dynamics.

## §5. The full sweep: one confirmation, one genuine discovery

**[THM, two-implemented] The cross-Gram condition is class-universal.** Swept across all 12 mixed-extreme-class diagonals, not just the one anchor tested in Rev 1.0: $L_c^TL_a+L_a^TL_c=0$ exactly, deviation $0.0$ on every single one. The mixed extreme class's full-band $J_0$-invariance is now a genuine class-theorem, matching G3's own kernel statement in scope.

**[FACT, two-implemented] The generic class's Lagrangian pairing is not universal — it splits $48/60$, exactly along a boundary this note did not anticipate.** Swept across all 60 generic diagonals: the pairing mechanism of §2 (band 2 and the coupled sector as Lagrangian duals) holds on 48 of them, and fails — cleanly, not by degree, deviation jumping from machine-zero to $2.83$ with nothing between — on the other 12. The failing 12 are exactly, and only, the anchors whose low-half index $i$ and high-half index $j-8$ satisfy $\{i,j-8\}\in\{\{1,5\},\{2,6\},\{3,7\}\}$ — Paper V's own $J_0$-partner pairs, the six index-pairs identified in Phase 5 Stage A as the mediator kite's own assessors ("$\Gamma_0\Gamma_4,\Gamma_1\Gamma_5,\Gamma_2\Gamma_6,\Gamma_3\Gamma_7$... the mediator-partner pairing"). Verified as an exact biconditional, no exception in either direction, across the full 60.

**What this means, stated at the weight it's earned.** The generic class is not mechanistically uniform the way the two extreme classes are. Something about an anchor's relationship to the mediator's own $J_0$-partner structure — not just whether the anchor *contains* $e_4$ or $e_{12}$ (which is what separates the three classes to begin with), but whether its two ingredient indices happen to *be* a partner pair *within* the generic class — changes which mechanism governs its branch correspondence. This is a real, precise, checkable fact.

**[THM, two-implemented] The mechanism, found and verified universally: the split is exactly the vanishing of the associator $(e_i,e_4,e_{j_{\rm low}})$.** Decomposing $[T_A,J_0]$ into its three pieces ($T_{e_i}$-alone, $T_{e_j}$-alone, and the cross-term $L_i^TL_j+L_j^TL_i$) isolates the entire effect in the cross-term: both single-generator pieces provably commute with $J_0$ regardless of partner status (a direct consequence of $e_i$'s own purely-antilinear behavior under G4), so any difference must live in how the two ingredients interact. It does, exactly: $\|[T_A,J_0]\|=8$ when the associator $(e_i,e_4,e_{j_{\rm low}})$ is nonzero, and $8\sqrt2$ exactly when it vanishes — confirmed as a strict biconditional across all 60 generic anchors, no exceptions. The associator vanishes precisely when $e_i,e_4,e_{j_{\rm low}}$ lie in a common associative (quaternionic) subalgebra — equivalently, when $e_i\cdot e_{j_{\rm low}}=\pm e_4$ directly (verified: $e_1\cdot e_5=-e_4$, $e_2\cdot e_6=-e_4$, $e_3\cdot e_7=-e_4$ exactly, and only for these pairs).

**This is not a new phenomenon local to this note — it is the redistribution operator's own associative/anti-associative band structure, one level up.** That companion document found the zero-divisor's own three-band spectrum splits along exactly this associator, vanishing identically on the associative middle band by Artin's theorem. The same non-associativity that organizes the algebra's own zero-divisor geometry is what decides, at the level of the dynamics built on top of it, whether an anchor's branch correspondence pairs across the seam or splits per band.

## §6. The third type: the mediator kite is a symplectic hybrid, not a failure

**The 12 "exceptions" of §5 are not broken instances of mechanism 1 — they are a third, distinct symplectic anatomy, in which both known mechanisms operate simultaneously on the same anchor, partitioned by band.**

**[THM, two-implemented] Hybrid statics.** For every mediator-kite diagonal ($e_i+e_j$ with $\{i,j-8\}$ a $J_0$-partner pair), independently verified across all 6 unique index-planes: the kernel and the $4$-band remain individually isotropic ($\|\Omega|_{\rm band0}\|,\|\Omega|_{\rm band4}\|\sim10^{-16}$), while band $2$ is genuinely $\Omega$-nondegenerate ($|\det(\Omega|_{\rm band2})|=1$ exactly), and the kernel–$4$-band cross-pairing is itself invertible ($|\det B_{04}|=1$ exactly). So band $2$ is a self-contained phase space (mechanism 2 applies to it alone), while the kernel and the $4$-band are Lagrangian duals of *each other* (mechanism 1's cross-formula applies between them) — one anchor, two mechanisms, cleanly separated by band.

**[THM, two-implemented] The dynamical composite, tested and confirmed exactly.** Integrating a kite diagonal's own flow ($e_1+e_{13}$, $\nu=1.5$, $t=3$) and comparing against the full system's actual measured branches ($\{3.354,806.358\}$): band $2$'s own restricted symplectic spectrum, evolved under its own dynamics alone, gives $806.358$ — one branch, exactly. The kernel–top pair's geometric mean ($\nu(t)^2=\mathrm{eig}(\sigma_0(t)\cdot B_{04}\cdot\sigma_4(t)\cdot B_{04}^T)$) gives $3.354$ — the other branch, exactly. The composite of the two local mechanisms reproduces the full system's spectrum completely; no branch is missed, no extra branch appears.

**The classification is now complete: three symplectic anatomies govern all 84 diagonals, one invariant deciding which.** $48$ generic diagonals pair fully across the seam (mechanism 1, whole-anchor); $24$ extreme-class diagonals ($12$ pure-antilinear, $12$ mixed) decompose into three fully independent per-band phase spaces (mechanism 2, whole-anchor); $12$ mediator-kite diagonals run both mechanisms at once, partitioned by band (the hybrid, this section). The deciding invariant throughout is the anchor's associator relationship with the mediator — vanishing on the extreme classes' own kernel by G3, vanishing or not within the generic class by §5's criterion, and this section's own case is the associator's vanishing on a *sub-part* of a generic-class anchor rather than the whole of it. **This split is invisible to the composition rule**: every one of these 60 non-extreme diagonals is class $(4,12)$ by G4 — the symplectic anatomy is strictly finer than the algebraic class, keyed to the same associator throughout.

**A useful reduction for the remaining open item.** $T_A-T_i-T_j=-\{L_i,L_j\}$ identically (verified across all tested anchors, following directly from $L_i^T=-L_i$ for any imaginary unit) — the cross-term driving §5's entire split is exactly $-\{L_i,L_j\}$, the mixed-half anticommutator. The still-open quantitative derivation (why associator-vanishing produces a factor of exactly $\sqrt2$ between the two commutator norms) reduces to computing $\|[\{L_i,L_j\},L_4]\|$ in the two associator regimes — a narrower, more tractable target than the original open question.

**[THM, two-implemented] The reduction is complete: the full computation lives entirely at the octonion level.** Writing $a=e_i$, $b=e_{j_{\rm low}}$, the $16\times16$ cross-term's off-diagonal blocks are exact octonion expressions: the upper-right block of $\{L_i,L_j\}$ is $-[L_a,R_b]\circ K$ ($K$ the octonion conjugation), with the symmetric lower-left block from the doubling formula's own counterpart. Carrying this through the further commutator with $L_4$ and comparing against the ground-truth $\|[T_A,J_0]\|$ directly: the two match exactly, in every tested case, both regimes ($8.0$ for non-partners, $8\sqrt2$ for partners) — the entire computation is now expressible without ever building a $16$-dimensional matrix.

**One correction along the way, recorded rather than silently fixed.** The formula first proposed for the upper-right block used $L_aR_b-R_bR_a$; this is a genuine transcription error, not merely a sign — tested directly against the actual block, it misses by a constant $4.90$ in every case (the residual itself a clean fingerprint, but of the wrong culprit). The correct expression uses the standard commutator $L_aR_b-R_bL_a$, verified to machine precision. This commutator is, in turn, exactly $-1$ times the associator operator itself ($[L_a,R_b]=-\text{associator}(a,\cdot,b)$, verified directly, matching the redistribution operator's own established identity) — so the reduction closes a genuine loop: the same associator that classifies which anchors are partners is, up to sign and conjugation, the literal operator computing the cross-term's own off-diagonal structure.

**What the reduction narrows the remaining question to, and one dead end recorded honestly.** The raw commutator $C:=[L_i,R_{j_{\rm low}}]$ is antisymmetric, rank $4$, and — in the tested non-partner case — confined entirely to the four-dimensional subspace $\{e_4,e_5,e_6,e_7\}$ (the mediator plus the $X$-triple), acting there as a clean rotation generator. Its own norm is identical, $4.0$, in both regimes — the $\sqrt2$ does not come from $C$'s magnitude, only from how it interacts with $L_4$ specifically. The natural-looking shortcut — restrict everything to $C$'s own invariant subspace and compute $\{C,L_4\}$ there — does **not** work: $L_4$ does not preserve $\{e_4,\ldots,e_7\}$ at all, mapping every element of it entirely outside (checked directly: $L_4(e_4)=-e_0$, $L_4(e_5)=e_1$, $L_4(e_6)=e_2$, $L_4(e_7)=e_3$, no component remaining in the subspace). An earlier attempt at this restriction appeared to give a clean zero, but the restricting matrix used was itself identically zero for this reason — not a structural fact, an artifact of the setup. Recorded as a checked, ruled-out path rather than erased, so it is not retried. The genuine closed-form derivation — computing the leakage term's exact contribution to $\{C,L_4\}$ analytically — remains open.

**One structural note worth stating plainly.** The mediator kite is now distinguished by the same underlying fact at three separate levels of this corpus: its assessors are the module's own $J_0$-partner pairs (Phase 5 Stage A); it is the unique kite that is class-pure under the composition rule (E3); and its diagonals are the unique symplectic hybrids (this section). All three are the same quaternionic relationship — $e_i,e_4,e_{j_{\rm low}}$ generating an associative subalgebra — read at three different levels of the construction.



Both mechanisms were originally verified on one representative anchor of the relevant class, not on all 84 by a general argument; §5 closed that gap for the cross-Gram condition and its mechanism, and §6 closes it completely for the remaining 12 — not exceptions to be explained away, but a third, fully classified symplectic type in their own right. All 84 diagonals now carry a proven symplectic anatomy: $48$ paired, $24$ per-band, $12$ hybrid, one invariant (the associator with the mediator) governing all three. What remains genuinely open, narrowed three times now: the precise quantitative derivation of the $\sqrt2$ ratio. The computation reduces completely to octonion-level operators (verified exact); the driving object $C=[L_i,R_{j_{\rm low}}]$ is confined to a $4$-dimensional subspace with regime-independent norm; and one natural shortcut (restricting the anticommutator with $L_4$ to that same subspace) is checked and ruled out, since $L_4$ does not preserve it. The closed-form step — the leakage term's exact contribution — is smaller than it has ever been and still not done.

## What died on the way here, kept on the record

This note's own first attempt at the extreme-class mechanism assumed a generic $8$-dimensional block-diagonal $\Omega$ for each drift-invariant sector, without checking whether that sector was symplectically closed at all — it wasn't, for the generic anchor, and the resulting spectrum was nonsensical (three values from an eight-dimensional space, no correspondence to anything measured). The error was caught by checking the assumption (is this subspace $\Omega$-invariant at all?) before trusting its output, the same countermeasure this season named after the stale-$J_0$-file arc. The corrected reading — that non-isotropy versus isotropy is itself the thing to test, not assumed away — is what produced both mechanisms above.
