## 2. The Gaussianity gate: construction and its two-implementation

**[FACT, two-implemented]** The candidate dissipative dynamics is built from linear jump operators on the anchor's own real-linear operator $L_A$, expressed in a mode basis built from $J_0=-L_{e_4}$ — $\mathbb{S}$'s own invariant complex structure, fixed by the mediator direction, distinct from any wall-sector or gauge-module object also carrying the name "$J_0$" elsewhere in the corpus. (This distinction is stated explicitly here because blurring it was the exact species that caused this arc's own stale-coordinate bug, §3 below; a note about that bug should not commit it.) In quadratures $r=(x_1,p_1,\ldots)$, each jump $L_j=c_j^Tr$, with $M=\sum_jc_jc_j^\dagger$, the covariance obeys the closed-form Lyapunov equation
$$\dot\sigma = A\sigma+\sigma A^T+D,\qquad A=-\Omega\,\mathrm{Im}(M),\quad D=\Omega\,\mathrm{Re}(M)\,\Omega^T,$$
$\sigma$ centered and symmetrized, $\Omega$ the standard symplectic form. This formula was independently derived and cross-verified against direct Fock-space simulation (finite truncation, exact master-equation dissipator), converging to machine precision as truncation grows — the formula itself was never in dispute once both sides tested it against ground truth rather than each other.

## 3. The arithmetic fork, and what it actually was

**[LEDGER, the arc's own record]** What followed was not a physics disagreement but a five-layer convention stack, each layer catching a genuine error before the next could hide behind it.

**Layer 1 — stale coordinates.** The anchor $L_A$ built fresh in this session's own basis was combined with a saved $J_0$ file from an earlier part of this long session, built on a silently different basis realization. Both objects passed every intrinsic check individually ($J_0^2=-\mathrm{Id}$ exact; $L_A$'s tunnel spectrum exact) — alignment between them was never tested until a full rebuild on consistent inputs was forced. Root cause named precisely: **saved-object coordinate provenance** — a file can carry validity without carrying the coordinate frame it was validated in, and two individually-correct objects from different moments of one session are not automatically combinable. Resolution: full rebuild from one `cd16` call, no saved files. Confirmed split: $\|C\|^2=4$, $\|D\|^2=12$, matching the independently-derived basis-free invariant ($\|L_c\|^2/2$, $\|L_a\|^2/2$ for the $J_0$-complex-linear/antilinear parts of $L_A$) exactly.

**Layer 2 — the definitional fork.** Two constructions for "the anchor's dynamics under the mirror orientation" were each built and verified exact: (a) the same quadrature machine fed the mirror's conjugated inputs, giving $A_2=A_1$ (orientation-blind) and $[P,A_1]=0$; (b) the literal adjoint jump-set $\{L_j^\dagger\}$, giving $A_2=-A_1$ (arrow-duality, loss$\leftrightarrow$gain). Both are exact theorems about two different, well-defined objects — not competing answers to one question. **[THM, resolved as a duality, not a choice]** The two compose: orientation-flip followed by the construction equals time-reversal of the construction. Neither reading is privileged; a future document naming "the mirror candidate" states locally which half it means.

**Layer 3 — a mislabeled scalar.** A proposed "kernel diffusion $=N(A)$" identity was traced to its source and found to be a Frobenius norm of a $4\times4$ block, reported as a per-direction rate. Rerun with the value computed two independent ways (via the assembled $D$ matrix, and directly as $\sum_j|c_j\cdot k|^2$) gives $k^TDk=1.0$ per kernel direction, both routes agreeing to machine precision. Bonus structural fact, confirmed: $\mathrm{Re}(M)$ annihilates the kernel directly — the jump vectors are exactly orthogonal to it — so the diffusion the kernel experiences arrives entirely through the $\Omega$-dressing, from its symplectic partner directions, not from any direct coupling.

**Species logged, this arc, both sides:** *saved-object coordinate provenance* (mine); *output-attribution blur*, *rationalized disconfirmation*, *theorem-scope inflation*, *quantity-type mislabeling* (F's, each independently self-diagnosed and ledgered in turn). The countermeasure common to all: a cheap, mechanical check (print the actual matrices consumed, not re-derived ones) run *before* trusting an internally-consistent-looking result, not after a contradiction forces it.

**Discriminator 1 (deepens vs. wanders), stated as result.** The expanding anchor's $\chi_{\rm kin}$ **deepens**: both branches grow monotonically, every successive state comparable and strictly deeper in Becoming's own partial order — genuine, coordinated, spectrum-valued depth-motion, off-spine, without ever reaching stationarity. ## 6. The frozen sector: identified, mechanism included

**[FACT, two-implemented]** The contracting anchor's flow carries a genuinely silent sector — four directions with both zero drift and zero diffusion, frozen at their initial value throughout the integration. A neutral scan against every natural candidate subspace (no favored hypothesis) identifies these exactly: **the frozen directions coincide precisely with $L_A$'s own kernel** — the seam note's tunnel — with maximum overlap ($2.0000$ against a $4$-dimensional space) and exact zero against every other candidate tested (the scalar span, the anchor plane, the strut fiber). The split against the $\tau$-halves is exactly equal, matching the tunnel lemma's own "perfectly $\tau$-balanced" description precisely. The expanding anchor carries no such sector at all — every one of its zero-drift directions carries identical, nonzero diffusion; the frozen phenomenon is not generic to the construction.

**[LEMMA, two-implemented] The mechanism.** For $k\in\ker L_A$, $L_ck=-L_ak$ always (since $L_c+L_a=L_A$ vanishes on the kernel) — but the kernel is $J_0$-invariant if and only if the stronger, joint condition holds: $L_ck=L_ak=0$ *separately*. Verified across all 84 canonical diagonals: at the pure-antilinear class ($C^2=0$), this is automatic; at the genuinely mixed extreme class ($C^2=12,D^2=4$ — *not* pure, both parts nonzero), the joint annihilation is a real, nontrivial fact, confirmed exactly ($\|L_c\cdot\ker\|=\|L_a\cdot\ker\|=0$ on all 12 members); at the generic middle class, it fails on all 60. **The seam note's own tunnel lemma — kernel commutes with neither $\tau$ nor $L_{e_4}$ — is generic, not universal**: the exceptional set is exactly these 24 extreme-class diagonals, both classes, not one.

**[FACT, two-implemented] The freeze is sign-blind by the shape of its own formula, not by a check that could have failed.** $J_0$ enters $L_c,L_a$ only sandwiched — $(-J_0)X(-J_0)=J_0XJ_0$ identically for any $X$ — so the split, the three classes, and the 24-anchor criterion could never have distinguished $J_0$ from $-J_0$ at any stage, before any anchor is even chosen. (An earlier framing of this as "the sanctuary's door is keyed to the mirror bit and happens to pass" is retired: the check that phrasing describes is a tautology for any subspace, structurally incapable of failing, and tests nothing anchor-specific. The correct, stronger statement is that nothing here was ever at risk.)

**[THM, two-implemented] The class-prediction is now a class-theorem.** A full sweep confirms both drift-freeze and diffusion-freeze on all 24 extreme-class anchors, not merely the one first tested (max residuals $5.7\times10^{-15}$ and $1.2\times10^{-16}$ across the full set). Separately, $\mathrm{Re}(M)$'s annihilation of the kernel is confirmed universal — true on all 84 diagonals, including the 60 generic ones — following directly from the jump vectors being built from $L_A$'s own rows, which the kernel kills by definition; the diffusion-freeze's restriction to exactly the 24 extreme-class anchors comes entirely from whether the kernel's symplectic partner ($\Omega^Tk$) itself stays inside a region $\mathrm{Re}(M)$ annihilates — which is exactly the $J_0$-invariance condition above.

**[FACT, two-implemented] Question (iv), answered honestly negative.** The scalar directions ($e_0$, $e_8$) were tested directly against each anchor's own drift/diffusion structure, no hypothesis presumed: both carry nonzero drift and nonzero diffusion in every anchor tested — confirmed on the two anchors of §5 at the time, and confirmed again, later, on the $(0,16)$ anchor of §6 once it existed — a representative of all three classes now checked, not merely two. **The dissipation does not conserve the scalar channel** — it engages the measurement channel exactly as it engages any generic direction, in every class tested. The kernel avoids the scalars statically (the scalar-avoiding kernel theorem stands, untouched); the dynamics does not.
